Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It's a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or sausage
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add chopped onion and garlic, and sauté until softened.
  4. Add ground beef or sausage to the skillet and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  5. Stir in tomato sauce, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  6. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  7. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Place a layer of cooked lasagna noodles on top.
  8. Spread a layer of ricotta cheese over the noodles, followed by a layer of mozzarella cheese.
  9. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 20-25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  11. Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
